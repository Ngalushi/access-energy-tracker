Progress to 300 million

FY26 was the best year yet at 28 million, but the yearly pace still has to rise by more than half, to about 44 million, and stay there until 2030. AfDB has 35 million more people expected from its current portfolio and needs about 10 million a year.

AfDB figures from its Mission 300 Progress Tracker launch (29 May 2026): 74 active projects and US$9 bn approved, of which US$6 bn is AfDB's own money. World Bank Group co-financing mobilised since the start of Mission 300: US$2.7 bn, led by EIB ($347 m) and AfDB ($161 m).

Questions for the World Bank and AfDB

  • Quality of connection: how many of the 64 m are grid, mini-grid or solar-kit connections, and at what service tier? The headline does not say.
  • Verified or estimated: the count uses household-size multipliers. Will verified household numbers be published alongside?
  • Credit for old money: connections from projects approved before 2023 count toward the 300 m. How much of the 64 m is from pre-Mission 300 projects?
  • Clean cooking: compacts include clean cooking, but the 300 m target is electricity only. Where is the cooking target and the money for it?
  • One tracker: the two banks report on different dates and platforms. Will they publish one joint, downloadable dataset?

What this means for the tracker

The new round of NDCs is the baseline to hold lenders to for 2025–2030. Countries still on 2020-era NDCs include Ghana, Tanzania, Uganda, DR Congo, Mali, Senegal, Sudan, Namibia and Egypt.

The next step is reading each new NDC for its electricity access, clean cooking and renewable targets, and linking them to the projects above.

What we could not see: data gaps that are themselves an accountability finding

81% of AfDB energy money is coded as generic "energy policy"AfDB's IATI records put most energy activities under one catch-all code (23111), so technology can only be found by reading titles and documents. We classified by title for this dashboard.
Hydropower coded as coal or non-renewable11 AfDB hydro and geothermal projects carry fossil-power CRS codes (23310/23320) in IATI. Code-only analysis would misreport them as fossil.
Only a third of AfDB commitments show disbursementsOf $11.3 bn committed, $3.7 bn is reported as spent; more than 4 in 10 activities report no disbursement at all, mostly private-sector loans.
World Bank sector coding missing for 2024 onwardOnly 15 of 144 World Bank SSA projects approved in 2024 carry sector shares in the public API, so 2024–25 (including Mission 300 operations) cannot yet be split by technology. We stopped the World Bank series at 2023.
IFC, MIGA and OECD CRS not yet includedIFC and MIGA use separate disclosure portals, and the OECD CRS and AfDB's own project portal could not be reached from our tools today. These are the next sources to add.
Household connections not yet availableFinanced vs verified connections need project results frameworks and completion reports. This needs document review, planned for the next phase.

Sources and method

Access: World Bank World Development Indicators API, 2015–2024, SSA aggregate and 48 countries.

World Bank finance: World Bank Projects API, Eastern and Southern plus Western and Central Africa regions, major sector "Energy and Extractives", board approval 2015–2023. Energy amount = current total IBRD/IDA commitment × energy sub-sector percentages. Mining excluded. Trust funds, IFC and MIGA not included.

AfDB finance: AfDB IATI data via d-portal (reporter XM-DAC-46002), energy CRS codes 23xxx, activities starting 2015–2025, North Africa and Djibouti excluded, commitment × sector share in US$. Year is activity start, not board approval.

Classification: World Bank sub-sectors mapped to Renewable, Fossil (Oil and Gas, Non-renewable generation), Grids (T&D), Policy (Public administration, energy) and Unclear (Other energy). AfDB classified by keywords in project titles because CRS codes were unreliable.

Access line: projects whose titles mention access, electrification, last mile, rural, off-grid, mini-grid or clean cooking. A first screen, not the full CAFOD/ODI seven-criteria tagging.

NDCs: Climate Watch (WRI) NDC indicators, 54 countries.

All amounts are current US$, not inflation-adjusted. Figures are first-pass estimates from public data and should be checked before publication.
